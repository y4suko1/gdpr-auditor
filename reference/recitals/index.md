# Recitals Index (Routed Subset)

Recitals are interpretive context for the GDPR's articles: they are NOT binding obligations themselves. Use them only when an article's wording is ambiguous and you need to understand its intent. Always cite the article, never the recital, as the source of a binding requirement.

**Status: partial by design.** This folder holds the full, verbatim text of the ~50 recitals that interpret the articles a privacy-policy audit actually routes to (see `../index.md` and `../../rules.md` section 1), not all 173. Nothing here is summarised or reworded; each file is the complete recital text, just a narrower set than the full Regulation.

**Not included here**: recitals interpreting articles outside the routed set (supervisory authority structure, the European Data Protection Board, penalties, member-state derogations, and similar provisions that don't bear on a privacy policy document), plus recitals that are purely legislative background (e.g. relationship to prior directives, TFEU mandate) rather than interpretive of an operative obligation.

If a routed article's wording is ambiguous and none of the recitals above seem to cover it, note the gap rather than assume no recital exists. This subset was mapped by topic, not verified exhaustively against every paragraph of every routed article.
